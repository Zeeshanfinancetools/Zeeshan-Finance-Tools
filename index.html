3. Understanding Loan Interest Rates & Monthly EMI Calculations

Equated Monthly Installments (EMI) allow borrowers to structure fixed payments toward outstanding mortgage or personal loans over time. The calculation involves three key components:

  • Principal Amount: The total loan amount borrowed from financial institutions.
  • Interest Rate: Annualized percentage rate charged on the principal.
  • Loan Term: Duration in months or years over which payments are spread out.

Using an online EMI calculator helps budget personal finances effectively without unexpected interest compounding surprises.
